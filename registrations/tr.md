# TR

*Registered 2026-11-23 · GPU runs · claim K1 · result 2026-11-25*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | Does the turn-release contrast (closed-loop turns stuck, open-loop turns recovered) hold against three objections: runs days apart on another kernel (arm O, open loop at λ = 2.0 rerun on the current stack), headroom (arm H, open loop at λ = 2.73, the closed arm's ratio of λ to warm capacity), and benchmark practice (arm D, each turn released at max(planned time, previous turn's end), AIPerf's default trace replay)? |
| **Runs** | 30 runs, 37.5 GPU h expected (≤ 112.5 h with three attempts each): L1, 350 W, 4080 s of arrivals, burst of 2.4 req/s at 900 s for 180 s, seeds 15–19; every burst run with its own no-burst control; arms O and H open loop, arm D with `workload.sessions.release: max_planned`. One queue, one seed at a time, arms rotated within seeds, after T1's queue on the same kernel. |
| **Outcome rule** | Per seed `k1.open_seed` on the windows [300, 900) and [3480, 4080) s with thresholds 0.06, 0.06 and 1.0. O, first match: `too_few` (n < 3), `pending_controls`, `lead_fails` (stuck + lost + cold control ≥ 3), `replicates` (every counted seed recovered), `mixed`. H: `cell_unstable` (control cold or partial in ≥ 3 of 5), `too_few`, `headroom_explains` (stuck + lost ≥ 3), `headroom_excluded` (no seed stuck or lost and ≥ 3 recovered; partial allowed, not counted), `mixed`. D: `too_few`, `pending_controls`, `default_sees` (stuck + lost + cold control ≥ 3), `default_blind` (every counted seed recovered), `mixed`. Overload and release-fault seeds are out of n. |
| **Prediction** | O: `replicates` 0.860, `mixed` 0.140. H: `mixed` 0.493, `headroom_excluded` 0.301, `cell_unstable` 0.163, `too_few` 0.027, `headroom_explains` 0.015. D: `mixed` 0.533, `default_sees` 0.440, `default_blind` 0.026. |
| **Decision under each outcome** | O `replicates` with the closed-loop rerun cold on the same stack: the paper's 'historical controls' becomes 'same stack, next day, not interleaved'. O `lead_fails`: the turn-release lead is withdrawn. H `headroom_excluded`: the open arm does not stick at the closed arm's warm-capacity ratio. H `headroom_explains`: the author words the K1 sentence with H's counts; H alone never withdraws the lead. D `default_sees` or `default_blind`: the benchmark sentence names AIPerf's default mode. Any other outcome: reported by counts. |
| **Adversarial review** | Two rounds (the limit). Round 1: five blocking items (a lost control left the verdict pending; consequences hung on a joint label; O's same-stack sentence needed the closed rerun; H's censored world had no label; H's failure label withdrew the lead alone) and seven minor ones, all applied. Round 2: one new blocking item (`headroom_excluded` counted partial recoveries), fixed before any data. |
| **Result** | O `replicates`: open-loop turns at λ = 2.0 recovered in 5 of 5 seeds on kernel 6.8.0-149 (lower bound 0.549), where the closed-loop rerun on the same stack (T1's U) was cold and backlogged in 5 of 5 (McNemar p = 0.031). H `headroom_excluded`: open loop at λ = 2.73 recovered in 5 of 5, every control warm, none stuck, lost or partial. D `default_sees`: with each turn released at max(planned time, previous turn's end), 5 of 5 seeds stuck (reuse 0.000, KV 0.95, 211–314 sessions in service at the end) beside 5 of 5 warm controls. Joint summary `lead_holds`. |
| **Prediction held** | O yes (`replicates` 0.860); H no (`headroom_excluded` 0.301, second to `mixed` 0.493; 0 of 5 controls out of n against a 0.32 prior); D no (`default_sees` 0.440, second to `mixed` 0.533) |
| **Not separated** | Registered as separable by no outcome: turn release against the bounded open population (9–13 sessions in service against 218–320); for H, warm headroom against the position between the cold floor and the warm capacity (the matched λ is 2.14–2.73 depending on the closed warm capacity used). |
| **Corrections** | The `headroom_excluded` rule was miscoded from a misread review option and fixed before any run. |
| **In the paper** | not yet in the paper |
| **Notes** | 30 of 30 runs valid on the first attempt. Arm A's runs of the same seeds also recovered, with end reuse equal to O's (same schedule and prompts by seed). H's shock (197–340 outstanding) was at least REG-19's (131–238). In D, 0.87–0.88 of later turns went out the moment their previous reply ended, and reuse stayed at 0.000, so zero-gap reuse did not rescue it. D bears on the population alternative but does not separate early release from the bounded population. |
