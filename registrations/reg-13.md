# REG-13

*Registered 2026-11-09 · GPU runs · result 2026-11-10*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | What load does the warm L1 server at 350 W carry with closed-loop turns, and is it at or above the planned load each cold run of REG-11 and REG-12 faced after its trigger (λp,max = 2.711)? Separates overload from a cold state that outlasts its load. |
| **Runs** | 5 valid runs, no rerun: `configs/sweep_l1pl350cl_pc_on.yaml`, cache on, closed-loop turns, `loadgen_force_close`, 350 W, no dev mode, seed 0, 900 s per rate with 240 s warm-up, rates 2, 2.4, 2.6, 2.8, 3 in one invocation. |
| **Outcome rule** | `gate.reg13_table` and `gate.reg13_outcome`. A rate passes if throughput ≥ 0.95 × planned rate and the server queue slope plus `gate.due_unreleased_slope` ≤ 0.01 req/s. μ_warm,cl: the highest rate passing with every lower rate (monotone); `carried`: the realized planned rate of its run. `collapsed` per failing rate: its first backlogged stretch starts ≥ 300 s after warm-up, ok completions before it ≥ 0.95 × planned session requests, and the counter hit rate after it ≥ 0.06 lower. Outcome in order: `incomplete`; `below` (`carried` ≥ 2.711); `collapsed`; `overload` (μ_warm,cl undefined or < 2.4); `partial`. |
| **Prediction** | 2.4 passes p ≈ 0.8; 2.6 p ≈ 0.5; 2.8 p ≈ 0.3; a failure at 2.4–2.8 is a collapse p ≈ 0.6. P(`below`) ≈ 0.38, `collapsed` ≈ 0.32, `partial` ≈ 0.15, `overload` ≈ 0.1, `incomplete` ≈ 0.05. Paired runs: throughput within 3 % of REG-8's at λ = 2 (p ≈ 0.85); at λ = 3 the closed-loop run fails (p ≈ 0.6). Each run valid p ≈ 0.9. |
| **Decision under each outcome** | `below`: draft C1's confirmation at REG-11's cell (a 4500 s watch, then the drop to λ = 1) and a note on C1's matrix wording. `collapsed`: the same confirmation run; the author decides whether the transition is the next measurement. `partial`: report `n_cold_below` of 7; the author chooses between the confirmation run and a lower-λ cell. `overload`: λ = 2.4 is beyond the closed-loop warm capacity, and REG-11's `pass` is not evidence of a metastable state; the author picks another factor or cell. `incomplete`: the author decides. |
| **Adversarial review** | One round of adversarial review: four blocking recommendations (a `collapsed` class, a registered basis of comparison, λp values fixed in code, a client-side backlog criterion for closed loop), all adopted before approval and the runs; recommendation 6 partly adopted, 7 and 8 adopted, 5 (a capacity check on existing runs at 2.4) not adopted. |
| **Result** | `overload`: μ_warm,cl is undefined, because λ = 2 fails the registered test only through the due slope (0.0195 > 0.01); n_cold_below 0. `collapsed` false at every rate (ok completions before the backlog 0.938, 0.932, 0.883 × planned at 2.6, 2.8, 3). By the configs' own rule alone (exploratory) μ = 2.4 (ratio 0.9503 against 0.95), which would have given `partial`. Paired with REG-8 at λ = 3: throughput 2.221 against 3.023. |
| **Prediction held** | no (`overload` had prior ≈ 0.1 against ≈ 0.38 for `below`) |
| **Not separated** | Overload (a slow deficit, the cache going cold as a consequence) against a cold switch at a surge (each failing rate went from hit ≈ 0.78 to 0 after surges of 3.33–3.61 in its window); separating them needs dropping the load after the switch. |
| **Corrections** | 2026-11-10: `gate.due_unreleased_slope` was not checked on a healthy closed-loop run before registration and fails one (λ = 2), measuring the schedule's ramp rather than a deficit; the registered outcome is unchanged. 2026-11-10: that correction's numbers recomputed with `gate.due_unreleased_profile` (later-turn planned rate 1.433 → 2.467 req/s); its 0.011 figure withdrawn. 2026-11-10: reading corrected; the runs at 2.6, 2.8 and 3.0 tipped after 120 s peaks of 3.42, 3.24 and 3.33, those at 2.0 and 2.4 peaked at 2.73 and 3.12 and did not tip, so 'μ_warm,cl ≈ 2.4' is not shown to be a sustained capacity; `overload` stands as computed. |
| **In the paper** | §4.1 (P2, the warm share; P7, the closed-loop warm side and its surges; text also in Appendix C.6); §4.1 and §6 (P25, K2's comparison uses its five cache-on runs); §4.6 (P42, its runs at 2.6 to 3.0 among the no-burst departures). Its prior is among those counted in Appendix D.2's calibration (P33). |
| **Notes** | The registered decision under `overload` reads REG-11's `pass` as not evidence of a metastable state; the later surge correction changes only the reading, not the computed outcome. |
