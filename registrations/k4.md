# K4: the surge test

*Registered 2026-11-17 · GPU runs · claim K4 · result 2026-11-17*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | At L1, 350 W, closed-loop turns, λ = 2.2, no trigger: does a schedule whose 120 s planned session rate first reaches 3.4 in [1200, 1800] s go cold within 212 s of that crossing and stay cold to 2700 s, and does a schedule that never reaches 3.1 stay warm? |
| **Runs** | Ten no-burst 2700 s runs on REG-14's control workload, seeds picked from the schedule alone: surge arm 133, 137, 175, 196, 287; calm arm 102, 103, 105, 106, 107; arms alternated in the run order; ≈ 9.5 GPU h. |
| **Outcome rule** | `k4.label`: a KV onset before 600 s is `tipped_early`; an onset in [600, 2700) is `tipped` (end window not recovered), `tipped_recovered` or `tipped_unclear`; without an onset `bad_baseline`, `warm` or `unclear`; a surge run is `on_time` if its onset is in [t_cross, t_cross + 212 s]. `k4.outcome` (`bad_baseline` left out of n): `too_few` (an arm below 4 runs); `refutes` (≥ 2 calm runs with any onset, or ≥ 2 surge runs `warm`); `supports` (≥ 4 surge `tipped`, no calm onset, ≥ 4 calm `warm`, ≥ 4 surge tips `on_time`; `supports_untimed` with fewer `on_time`); `mixed` otherwise. |
| **Prediction** | `supports` (both kinds) 0.467, `mixed` 0.401, `refutes` 0.133; per run surge `tipped` 0.75, calm `warm` 0.88, `on_time` given `tipped` ≈ 0.85 (BELIEF). |
| **Decision under each outcome** | `supports`: K4's status becomes judged, with the registered sentence (the edge within 3.1–3.4, the λ dependence and the spike against the few-minute rise left open); `refutes` from the calm arm: K4 leaves the claims; from the surge arm with only the marginal seeds warm: reworded to an edge above 3.4; `mixed` or `too_few`: K4 stays exploratory. |
| **Adversarial review** | One round: four blocking and four advisory changes; all applied before registration, the sixth (a test) in part. |
| **Result** | `supports`: 4 of 5 surge-arm runs tipped on time (lags 32–79 s) and stayed cold, 5 of 5 calm-arm runs stayed warm; the one warm surge run is the marginal seed 287 (5 s at or above 3.4). Calm tipped 0 of 5 (upper bound 0.451), surge not tipped 1 of 5 (upper bound 0.657); Fisher's one-sided p 0.024 (a readout). |
| **Prediction held** | yes (likeliest outcome, 0.467) |
| **Not separated** | A few-minute rise in planned load against a 120 s spike; a surge of new sessions against one of requests; where the edge lies (3.1–3.4 untested); a co-tenant's load on the host; chance with five runs per arm. |
| **In the paper** | The surge test: §4.6 and §6 (P44), Appendix C.16. |
| **Notes** | The feasibility gate passed before registration (surge 6 of 500 seeds qualify, calm 288 of 500). Departure from the claim's text: one calm onset gives `mixed`, not refutation. All ten runs were valid on their first attempt. The band between 3.1 and 3.4 was tested later by K4e (the band test). |
