# REG-22: the four-links reading

*Registered 2026-11-13 · a reading of runs already made · claim C2 · result 2026-11-13*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | In the burst runs that the registered verdict calls `stuck`, do condition 4's registered levels hold at the end of the watch (mean KV ≥ 0.80, mean running batch N ≥ 2 × baseline, N below the 256 cap), and do the loop's four links (cache lost, prompts recomputed, large batch, full pool) hold together in every 300 s span once no burst request is left in the system? |
| **Runs** | Batch 1: the 13 closed-loop L1 burst runs at 350 W made before REG-19 (λ = 2.4: REG-11, REG-12 seeds 2–6; λ = 2.2: REG-14, REG-15; λ = 2.0: REG-17, REG-18 seeds 3–6), 11 `stuck` and 2 `bad_baseline` (not judged). Batch 2: REG-19's five burst runs, seeds 15–19, λ = 2.0. 0 GPU h. |
| **Outcome rule** | `c2.condition4`, in order: `undefined` (levels unreadable); `not_memory_limited` (levels fail); `undefined` (fewer than two judged spans, or no exit); `not_sustained` (a judged span fails a link); `undefined` (a missing value); else `loop`. Links per 300 s span (`c2.links`): reuse of later turns ≤ baseline − 0.06; session uncached prefill ≥ 2 × baseline; mean N ≥ 2 × baseline; mean KV ≥ 0.80. A span is judged when it starts at or after the last burst request's end. Counted per load as k of n with one-sided 95 % Clopper–Pearson bounds; a sentence about a load needs at least 3 stuck runs. |
| **Prediction** | Batch 1 (11 stuck runs): `loop` in 9 to 11, all eleven ≈ 0.8; `not_sustained` in 0 to 2; `not_memory_limited` in 0; `undefined` in 0 to 1. Batch 2, if computed (P ≈ 0.6) and with at least three stuck runs (P ≈ 0.7 of that): a stuck run `loop` with probability about 0.96. Onsets: (reuse, N) and (reuse, KV) `reversed` in most stuck runs (≥ 8 of 11 ≈ 0.7). |
| **Decision under each outcome** | Computing and reporting only; each line a ceiling. a: `loop` in more than half of the stuck runs at a load (n ≥ 3) allows at most "condition 4's registered levels held … in k of n stuck runs", with the order clause unjudged and "an overload gives the same picture"; D1 and D2 may then be proposed. b: λ = 2.4 is the overload comparison, never cited as support. d: `not_memory_limited` with KV < 0.80 in more than half → "against C2 for that load", D1 and D2 not proposed as planned. e: `not_sustained` in more than half → the confirmation run must read every span. |
| **Adversarial review** | Twelve rounds of adversarial review on drafts 3 to 17, each NOT READY, with separate approval passes; ended by a review-stop rule fixed before any batch existed, under which batch 1 is the reading reported first and batch 2 is labelled retrospective by design. Every item of the last round was applied before batch 1 was computed (draft 18). |
| **Result** | Batch 1: `loop` in 4 of 4 stuck runs at λ = 2.0 (bounds 0.473 to 1.000) and 5 of 5 at λ = 2.4 (0.549 to 1.000); the two λ = 2.2 runs `loop`, reported singly; 54 judged spans, none failing a link. Batch 2: `loop` in 5 of 5 stuck runs at λ = 2.0 (0.549 to 1.000), 45 judged spans, none failing. Both batches meet line a at λ = 2.0 and do not disagree; each burst run's same-seed control (REG-15 has none) held the links in no judged span. |
| **Prediction held** | yes (likeliest outcome) in both batches: all eleven `loop` ≈ 0.8; batch 2's five ≈ 0.82 (DERIVED in the result from 0.96 per run) |
| **Not separated** | An overload gives the same picture (only PROTOCOL §3 conditions 2, 5 and 6 and the loop-cut controls decide it); the preemption cycle of Ao et al. is not separated; a `reversed` onset pair says nothing about when the cache was lost (late first tokens). |
| **In the paper** | §4.5, P34; App. C.14, P35 (λ = 2.4); §4.3, P36 (the onset order) |
| **Notes** | Batch 1's thresholds were fixed after its runs were reported, and most of its values were already published. Batch 2 is retrospective by design: before the registration a script had most likely printed two end-of-run request counts of REG-19's seed-17 burst run. The order clause of PROTOCOL §3 condition 4 is not judged; onsets are reported only (the run-level reading `differs` in every stuck run). |
