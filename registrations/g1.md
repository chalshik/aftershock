# G1: the second-schedule sweep

*Registered 2026-11-21 · GPU runs · result 2026-11-22*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | Are the default-budget cold floor, warm capacity and the cell at λ = 2.0 the same on a second workload schedule (workload seed 1)? |
| **Runs** | 12 runs (of the shared registration's 16, ≈ 5.4 GPU h in all): T4's 2048-token arm (pool pinned at 6041 blocks; cache off at 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4; cache on at 2.0–3.0 in 0.2 steps; 900 s per rate, a fresh server per run) with top-level `seed: 1`, which seeds the workload; the server's `--seed 0` unchanged. Compared with T4's 2048 arm on seed 0. Configs `configs/sweep_g1_l1pl350cl_pc_{off,on}_b2048_s1.yaml`. |
| **Outcome rule** | `g1.outcome`, with `t4.floor`, `t4.shared_floors`, `t4.warm_capacity`, `t4.label_cell`. delta = floor_s1 / floor_s0 − 1; `seed_same` if \|delta\| ≤ 0.03; warm capacities compared exactly on the 0.2 grid. Decision in order: `no_floor` → `no_warm` → `reference_moved` (the seed-0 cell fails) → `cell_seed_bound` (the seed-1 cell fails, the seeds differ beyond the band or in warm capacity, and `day_confounded` is clear) → `cell_edge` (the seed-1 cell fails otherwise) → `seed_stable` (both cells hold, `seed_same`, same warm capacity) → `seed_varies`. `day_confounded`: a G2 twin missing; or \|drift\| > 0.03 at either rate; or \|delta\| > 0.03 with the largest \|drift\| ≥ \|delta\| / 2 (drift = G2's mode-off twin / T4's same-rate run − 1). |
| **Prediction** | Floor within ±3 % of 1.789, P 0.75. `seed_stable` 0.40, `seed_varies` 0.52, `cell_seed_bound` 0.03, `cell_edge` 0.02, `no_floor`/`no_warm` 0.03 (restated from 0.55 and 0.37 after T4's warm-edge readout, before G1's runs). |
| **Decision under each outcome** | `seed_stable`: the floor, warm capacity, ratio and margin are printed as two-seed ranges; 'one seed each' leaves those sentences. `seed_varies`: the same, with the spread named (with `day_confounded`, said to include day-to-day change). `cell_seed_bound`: the margin claim is restated as seed-dependent. `cell_edge`: the cell is said to sit at its floor-side edge on one seed. `no_floor`, `no_warm`: the missing rate rerun once under the stopping rule, else reported. No outcome changes P0, C1, K1–K4 or any verdict. |
| **Adversarial review** | One round, shared by G1 and G2, before registration: four blocking and ten advisory items, all handled; two not applied as proposed (G-9: the caveat's deletion narrowed to the floor side instead of adding a cache-on pair; G-10b: the mode-off twins not moved, so that G2's pairs are not split). No second round. |
| **Result** | `seed_varies`: cold floors (shared rule from λ_sat 2.5, n 4) 1.789 on seed 0 and 1.819 req/s on seed 1, delta +0.0165 (`seed_same`); warm capacities 2.2 (seed 0) and 2.8 (seed 1); both cells hold (margins 1.118 and 1.112); `day_confounded` clear (drift +1.19 % at λ = 3, −0.25 % at λ = 4). |
| **Prediction held** | yes (likeliest outcome: `seed_varies` at 0.52; the floor held within ±3 %); seed 1's warm capacity of 2.8 was outside the predicted 2.2 or 2.4 |
| **Not separated** | Whether the keep-up rule measures a schedule's surges rather than a mean capacity; a lighter draw on average; day or host state on the warm side (no cache-on twin); chance (one run per rate). |
| **In the paper** | Appendix A, 'the second-schedule sweep'; §4.1 (P65); the surge and keep-up reading, read afterwards, §4.1 (P66). |
| **Notes** | One registration with G2; G1 ran because T4's 2048 cell held, and was computed after G2's result, as registered. A reading of seen runs that was not registered (A28) found that in 11 of 12 cache-on 2048 runs on the two seeds the engine kept up exactly when its schedule had no 120 s planned surge ≥ 3.4 req/s; seed 1's draws were 1.5–2.5 % lighter than nominal. The paper prints the closed-loop capacities as two-schedule ranges. |
