# T4: the step-budget sweep

*Registered 2026-11-21 · GPU runs · result 2026-11-21*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | Does the scheduler's step budget (vLLM's default 2048 tokens against 8192) move the cold floor, the warm capacity, or the cell at λ = 2.0? |
| **Runs** | 24 runs, ≈ 8.4 GPU h, seed 0, 900 s per rate, a fresh server per run, L1, 350 W, closed-loop turns: cache off at 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4 req/s and cache on at 2.0–3.0 in 0.2 steps, each at 2048 and at 8192 (`--max-num-batched-tokens 8192`), the pool pinned in both arms (`--num-gpu-blocks-override 6041`, 96,656 tokens); the two budgets adjacent at each rate, their order alternating. Configs `configs/sweep_t4_l1pl350cl_pc_{off,on}_b{2048,8192}.yaml`. |
| **Outcome rule** | `t4.outcome`. Each arm's cold floor by E1's rule (`t4.floor`, fallback `t4.floor_throughput`); Δ = floor₈₁₉₂ / floor₂₀₄₈ − 1 over the shared saturated rates: `neutral` if \|Δ\| ≤ 0.03, `floor_up` if Δ > 0.03 or the 8192 floor is `above_grid`, `floor_down` if Δ < −0.03. Warm capacity by the monotone keep-up rule (`warm_same`, `warm_up`, `warm_down` on the 0.2 grid). Cell (`t4.label_cell`): `cell_holds` iff 2.0 ≥ 1.05 × that arm's floor and 2.0 < its warm capacity. Decision in order: `no_floor` → `no_warm` → `baseline_moved` (the 2048 cell fails) → `cell_moves` (the 8192 cell fails on its floor side with `floor_up` or on its warm side with `warm_down`) → `cell_edge` (the 8192 cell fails otherwise) → `budget_neutral` (`neutral` and `warm_same`) → `budget_moves`. |
| **Prediction** | Δ ≈ +2 % (90 % range −3 to +6 %): `neutral` 0.55, `floor_up` 0.40, `floor_down` 0.05. Warm capacity: `warm_same` 0.60, `warm_up` 0.20, `warm_down` 0.20. Decision: `budget_neutral` 0.30, `budget_moves` 0.52, `cell_moves` 0.09, `cell_edge` 0.03, `baseline_moved` 0.03, `no_floor` 0.02, `no_warm` 0.01. |
| **Decision under each outcome** | `budget_neutral`, `budget_moves`: one sentence in the paper with Δ and both capacities; no further run. `cell_moves`: the paper says P0's cell depends on the scheduler's step budget, with both floors and capacities, and whether P0 is tested at 8192 is drafted for the author as a new registration; nothing starts by itself. `cell_edge`: reported as an edge, no further run. `baseline_moved`: reported as a finding about the setup. `no_floor`, `no_warm`: reported, no rerun beyond the stopping rule. |
| **Adversarial review** | Two rounds (the limit) before registration: round 1 READY with fixes (items T4-1 to T4-13, applied); round 2 READY with fixes, two blocking items (T4-R2-1: `cell_moves` only when the budget's own move breaks the cell; T4-R2-2: the fallback floor) fixed. Some round-2 items were left open for the author, and round 2's fixes were not reviewed again. |
| **Result** | `cell_moves`: cold floors 1.789 (2048) and 1.931 (8192) req/s, λ_sat 2.5 and n 4 in both, Δ = +0.0792 (`floor_up`); warm capacities 2.2 and 2.4 (`warm_up`; both at the keep-up rule's edge at 2.4, 0.9497 and 0.9527 of planned). The 2048 cell holds (2.0 = 1.118 × its floor, 2.0 < 2.2); the 8192 cell fails on the floor side (2.0 = 1.036 × 1.931 < 1.05). |
| **Prediction held** | no (`cell_moves` had 0.09, the third named outcome; `budget_moves` was likeliest at 0.52) |
| **Not separated** | One schedule (seed 0) is open: the 8192 cell fails by 1.4 %, inside the 3 % band, and no registered run tests it on another seed. The window rule is a registered heuristic; T4 ran no burst, so it does not say whether a burst at 8192 leaves the engine stuck. |
| **In the paper** | Appendix A, 'the step-budget sweep'; §4.1 (P62). |
| **Notes** | Time drift largely ruled out: the 8192 arm is higher at each of the four saturated rates (+7.1 to +9.0 %), and the 2048 floor matches E1's 1.798 within 0.5 %. Unpinned, the 8192 server would have had 5850 blocks (−3.2 %). The registered `cell_moves` decision led to P8192's registration; T4's 2048 values were filled into G1 and G2's registration. |
