# T2: the cache-off burst control

*Registered 2026-11-21 · GPU runs · claim C2 · result 2026-11-21*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Question** | With caching off, at D2's matched load λ = 0.80 req/s, does REG-19's burst leave a lasting line or throughput shortfall? |
| **Runs** | 6 runs, ≈ 4.6 GPU h: per seed 15, 16, 17, REG-19's burst config and its no-burst twin, changed only to caching off (`--no-enable-prefix-caching`), `workload.rate` 0.8, `duration_s` 2580 and `verdict.watch_s` 1500; the burst is 180 s of one-off turn-0 requests at 2.4 req/s (2944-token prompts) from 900 s; L1, 350 W, closed-loop turns, 8 per session. Order: control 15, burst 15, burst 16, control 16, control 17, burst 17. Configs `configs/t2_{burst,control}_l1pl350cl_off_r0.8_s{15,16,17}.yaml`. |
| **Outcome rule** | `t2.label_twin`, `t2.label_burst`, `t2.outcome`. Windows B = [300, 900), E = [1980, 2580) with halves E1, E2; q the mean waiting line, c session completions ok per s, p session requests planned per s. Baseline healthy iff q_B ≤ 1.0 and c_B ≥ 0.95 × p_B, else `bad_baseline`. Twin `drained` iff q_E ≤ q_B + 1.0 and c_E ≥ 0.95 × p_E. Burst run, with lined = q_E > q_B + 1.0 and short = c_E < 0.95 × the twin's c_E, in order: `drained` (neither), `unclear`, `draining` (lined, q_E2 < q_E1), `slow` (lined and short, q_E2 ≥ q_E1), `relapse`, `unclear`. A pair counts when both runs are valid, both baselines healthy and the twin `drained`. Outcome: `too_few` (n < 2), `slow_found` (any counted burst `slow`), `drains` (every counted burst `drained`), `mixed`. |
| **Prediction** | Per burst run: `drained` 0.93, `unclear` 0.03, `draining` 0.02, `slow` 0.015, `relapse` 0.005; per twin `drained` 0.95. Drain ≈ 275 s after the burst's end. Outcome: `drains` 0.79, `mixed` 0.135, `slow_found` 0.045, `too_few` 0.03. |
| **Decision under each outcome** | `drains`: C2's caching-off clause and §5.7 gain a run beside the arithmetic; no study decision changes. `slow_found`: removes the claim that a cache is necessary for a slow state after this burst (it would not show that REG-19's state is not cache-driven); the author decides what follows. `mixed`: reported per seed. `too_few`: reported with the stop's reason, no rerun beyond the stopping rule. |
| **Adversarial review** | Two rounds (the limit) before registration: round 1 NOT READY with six blocking items, all handled (among them a validation of the labels on REG-19's five pairs, pass condition ≥ 4 of 5 bursts `slow` and ≥ 4 of 5 twins `drained`, which passed 5 of 5 and 5 of 5); round 2 READY with two blocking fixes (`slow` tested before `relapse`; a missing half-window is `unclear`), applied and not reviewed again. |
| **Result** | `drains`: in 2 of 2 counted seeds (16, 17) the burst run and its twin were `drained`; drain times 338 and 390 s; `drained` 2 of 2 (one-sided 95 % lower bound 0.224), `slow` 0 of 2 (upper bound 0.776). Seed 15 not counted: `bad_baseline` in both runs (c/p 0.945 in B against the 0.95 bar; 431 completions against 456 planned). |
| **Prediction held** | yes (likeliest outcome: `drains` at 0.79); the drain times were 23–42 % longer than the predicted ≈ 275 s |
| **Not separated** | Headroom: the load was 0.44 of the cold floor (0.83 of μ_off) against 1.11 at the cache-on cell, so a drain is what the arithmetic predicts and does not show that a cache is necessary for REG-19's state. A latency-only slow state is not judged, and one that shows only after 2580 s is not tested. |
| **In the paper** | Appendix A, 'the cache-off burst control'; §4.5 (P63). |
| **Notes** | The run reverses the author's 2026-11-14 ruling that C2's caching-off control needed no run. Two counted seeds, not the registered three, so the bounds are wider than the registered 0.37 and 0.63; seed 15's miss lies in its schedule's window (identical c_B and p_B in both runs), and the rule was not widened. Burst KV reached 0.9993–0.9998, so the registered risk of failing the burst KV check did not occur. |
